1. About the app

Clip Maker is a personal desktop application for Windows, developed and used by Rafael Vallais only. It turns the user's videos into vertical clips and can send them as drafts to the user's own TikTok account. It is free, not sold and not offered to the public.

2. Use of TikTok

  • Clip Maker uses TikTok's official APIs (Login Kit and Content Posting API) only after the user connects their TikTok account and grants permission.
  • Clip Maker only uploads videos that the user explicitly chooses, as drafts in the user's TikTok inbox. The user reviews, edits and publishes each video themselves in the TikTok app.
  • The user remains responsible for the content they publish and must follow TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines, including copyright rules.
